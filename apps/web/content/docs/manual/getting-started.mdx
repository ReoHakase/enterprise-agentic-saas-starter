## Create an account

Sign in through the application and complete the account flow. Access to organization data is determined by your current membership and permissions.

## Choose an organization

Open the organization area and select the workspace you want to use. Keep work in the organization that owns the relevant issues and files.

## Work with issues

Use the issue workspace to search, create, update, and review issues that you are authorized to access. Confirm the organization and the target resource before making a change.

## Keep credentials private

Do not paste passwords, access tokens, private URLs, or other credentials into issues, attachments, prompts, or external tools.
