> Draft — this page is provided for review and does not replace an approved legal privacy policy.

## Scope

This draft describes the categories of information that may be processed when using Enterprise SaaS, including account information, organization membership, issue content, attachments, and integration requests.

## Use of information

Information may be used to provide the workspace, enforce organization access, process issue and file operations, protect the service, and operate supported integrations.

## Integrations

When an external MCP client is authorized, the service uses the granted scopes, the connected organization, and current membership and permission to determine which operations are available.

## Review status

Retention periods, legal bases, subprocessors, data-subject rights, contact details, and jurisdiction-specific terms require product and legal review before this page can be treated as a final policy.
